The NTP poll time should not be set to a multiple of 60 seconds. That will help spread out the load on the NTP servers. 7261 seconds is good, 7200 is not. If using an ntp.org pool server, polls should happen no more often than every 1/2 hour (1800 seconds) to comply with their terms of service. Start by setting the polling interval to a high value then observe how well the clock stays in sync. If it gets more than a second out of sync during that period, lower it. NTP providers will thank you. Regardless of the polling period, the clock will be sent a time update every second.
